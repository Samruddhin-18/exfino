How it works

Get started in minutes

Four simple steps to take control of your spending.

  1. 1

    Create a free account

    Sign up with your name and email — no credit card, no subscriptions. Your data stays private and secure.

  2. 2

    Log your expenses

    Hit + Add Expense, enter the amount, pick a category (Food, Bills, Transport…), choose the date, and optionally add a note. Done in seconds.

  3. 3

    See where your money goes

    Your profile shows a live summary — total spent, number of transactions, and a category breakdown with progress bars so you can spot patterns instantly.

  4. 4

    Filter by any time period

    Use the preset buttons (This Month, Last 3 Months…) or pick a custom date range to zoom in on any period and track trends over time.
